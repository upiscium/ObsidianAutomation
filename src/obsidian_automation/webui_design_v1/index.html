Status guide

✓ Succeeded verified outcome · ! Failed confirmed failure · ? Unknown no verified outcome.

Running / Queued / Idle describe execution, not success. Enabled / Disabled describe scheduling, not service health. Stopped is not inferred from an idle process. B5 is manual only.

Connected means an adapter observation, not permission to run, a working credential, or proof of a Vault change. Global health remains unknown without independent evidence.
